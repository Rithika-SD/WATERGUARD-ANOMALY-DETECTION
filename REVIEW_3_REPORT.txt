SECTION 1: PROBLEM STATEMENT AND PROJECT OBJECTIVE
In coastal flood-prone towns, apartment water leaks and abnormal consumption frequently go undetected until monthly billing cycles. Tens of thousands of liters of treated water are lost, building foundations suffer dampness/seepage, and residents face severe bill shocks.

WaterGuard is a field-ready smart building platform for small housing societies. It processes 15-minute sub-meter interval readings across apartment zones (Kitchen, Bathroom, Utility, Common Area, Water Tank, Plumbing Shafts) to detect leaks before monthly billing, localise probable leak sources with explainable evidence, integrate coastal flood risk indicators (CFI), and enforce human-in-the-loop staff verification.

SECTION 4: ERROR ANALYSIS AND LIMITATIONS
The evaluation engine analyzes failure modes:
1. False Positives (5 alerts): Multi-appliance concurrent water usage (e.g. washing machine + dishwasher during weekend mornings).
2. False Negatives: Extremely low-flow micro-drips (<0.5 L/h) falling below Z-score sensitivity floor.
3. Difficult Cases: Slow toilet flap leaks masked during high morning peak activity hours.
4. Limitations: Isolation Forest trained on synthetic data; raw sub-meters measure liters per interval, not direct physical channel debris or raw tidal height.
