1. PROBLEM STATEMENT & MOTIVATION
================================================================================
In coastal towns preparing for seasonal flooding and drainage blockages, water 
leaks and abnormal consumption in apartment complexes are often noticed only 
after receiving monthly utility bills. By that time:
  - Thousands of liters of treated water have been wasted.
  - Structural building foundations suffer dampness and seepage damage.
  - Residents face severe financial bill shocks.

Existing smart building solutions rely on expensive cloud infrastructure and 
unexplainable black-box AI models that are impractical for small municipal or 
apartment management teams with limited technical staff.

4. DATASET & GROUND TRUTH GENERATION
================================================================================
Due to privacy restrictions on real apartment meter data, a reproducible 
synthetic dataset generator (Seed: 42) was created:
  - Total Apartments: 80 across 4 building blocks (B1-B4, 5 floors each)
  - Sub-meter Zones: Bathroom, Kitchen, Utility, Common Area, Water Tank, Plumbing
  - Interval Duration: 15 minutes (96 intervals/day for 90 days)
  - Total Data Volume: 703,582 records
  - Injected Anomalies: Toilet Leakage, Pipe Leakage, Night Flow, Sudden Spike, 
    Gradual Increase, Sensor Stuck Anomaly, Legitimate High Usage (Party/Event).

================================================================================
5. BASELINE VS PROPOSED ANOMALY METHODOLOGY
================================================================================
BASELINE DETECTOR (Simple Rule-based):
  - Flags flow > 3x historical mean OR night consumption > 5L.
  - Results: Precision: 62.0%, Recall: 71.0%, F1-Score: 0.66, FPR: 24.0%.

PROPOSED EXPLAINABLE PIPELINE (35% Implementation):
  - Combines occupancy scaling, Z-score deviation, consecutive night-flow 
    counters, and coastal flood risk weighting.
  - Generates clear text evidence for every alert (e.g., "Night flow > 5L for 4 
    consecutive intervals; 2.7x above baseline").
  - Pre-billing KPI Target: ≥ 80% of leaks detected before monthly billing.
  - Measured Result: 88.5% (PASS) | Precision: 84.0% | Recall: 89.0% | F1: 0.86.
